\noindent The revised manuscript is a fixed-seed, single-step \emph{negative feasibility benchmark}. The current changes explain the recorded simulation rather than substituting hypothetical Qiskit results. ``Addressed'' below means the manuscript wording or computational diagnostic has been revised; it does not mean hardware validation or third-party auditing was performed.

\section*{Primary technical points}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Sampling model: addressed at the level of executable probability distributions and compatible circuit primitives.} The revised Section 3 explicitly specifies the complex-Pauli importance distribution $|c_\ell|/\Gamma_M$, the ideal Hadamard-test outcome $Z_a=\Gamma_M H_\ell$, and an independently sampled denominator $Z_D=\Gamma_K\operatorname{sgn}(d_j)Q_j$ from a \emph{separately decomposed} $K=M^\dagger M$. It gives the exact expectation and per-outcome variances $\Gamma_M^2-a^2$ and $\Gamma_K^2-D^2$. The real-Jacobian odd-$Y$ coefficients are retained by conditional phase adjustment. The numerator uses 200 / 864 nonzero terms and the denominator 136 / 528 on the two networks. A supplementary numerical check agrees with the predicted means and variances. The conditional circuit is a specification, not an executed Qiskit implementation; state preparation, controlled operations and measurement latency remain uncosted.

\item \textbf{Signed amplitude reconstruction: addressed.} The coherent branch state $(|0\rangle|i\rangle+|1\rangle|v\rangle)/\sqrt2$ and ancilla-$X$ measurement yield the \emph{signed} $v_i$, rather than computational-basis probabilities $|v_i|^2$. The same controlled-preparation phase convention must be applied to the signed numerator overlap. The archive samples each physical coordinate independently from $\operatorname{Binomial}(N_i,(1+v_i)/2)$; there is no renormalization, no measurement of padded coordinates and no statevector-sign copy into reconstructed outputs. Holdout returns undefined for $\widehat D\le0$; a prospective safe-abstention policy is now distinguished from the archived behaviour. All 24 archived holdouts reported a positive denominator confidence-lower-bound flag. A realized controlled-preparation circuit remains to be tested.

\item \textbf{Floating preconditioner: corrected.} The theorem treats $B_0=\operatorname{fl}(J(\widehat x)^{-1})$ as the exact matrix of stored binary numbers and validates $I-B_0J$ using directed arithmetic. It is \emph{not} required to enclose the exact inverse. Classical inversion and its cost remain explicitly disclosed.

\item \textbf{Runtime conclusion: narrowed.} The revised text states that classical factorization is not avoided in \emph{this} measured workflow, while a universal impossibility claim is not supported because candidate quality, number of nonlinear steps, certification calls and hardware runtimes differ across methods. The 1 ns/outcome illustration is explicitly unrealistically favourable to quantum devices.

\item \textbf{Checker independence: documented.} A new table lists the shared data, candidate states and preconditioner on one hand, and separately implemented decimal parsing, interval arithmetic, trigonometric enclosures, Jacobian/inclusion code and IEEE 9-bus constraint checks on the other. Agreement of positive inclusions is distinguished from negative sufficient-test flags. This is not external third-party code review.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Experimental and presentation points}
\begin{enumerate}\setcounter{enumi}{5}
\item \textbf{Scope and comparators: clarified.} The study comprises one sampled VQLS correction from each fixed warm start, exact undamped one-step Newton, a deliberately damped LU \emph{diagnostic}, and fully converged Newton. The damped LU is not called cost- or accuracy-matched. For the archived 24 quantum cases, the VQLS residual exceeded $\|b\|_\infty$ zero times; thus the specified damping ratio did not clip the residual target. The mixed-angle/voltage coordinates of the reported $\ell_\infty$ state distance are explicitly identified.

\item \textbf{Observed counts and radius geometry: improved.} All 24 individual trials appear in the new figure and geometry CSV. Three 9-bus base trials have positive root inclusions, three 14-bus base trials have a numerical reference root inside the largest tested box but no inclusion, and 18 stressed trials have the \emph{numerical reference root} outside the largest test box. The last category does not rule out a different nonlinear root. All fractions are observed counts, not empirical population success probabilities.

\item \textbf{Mathematical precision: improved.} The root-enclosure statement uses $\forall p\in\mathcal P,\exists! x_p^*\in\mathcal X$ with a differentiability neighbourhood assumption. The direct-centre constraint allowance is defined, and the standard nature of the Krawczyk argument remains explicit. Native security is claimed only for small-uncertainty IEEE 9-bus base cases; IEEE 14-bus is a root-inclusion test only.

\item \textbf{Reproducibility and presentation: improved but not complete.} The package contains the code-defined benchmark inputs, scenarios and outages, fixed-seed records, all-seed diagnostic CSV, probability-sampler tests, results and a claim manifest. The figure no longer connects categorical scenarios as continuous curves. The latest manuscript is a self-contained research paper rather than a revision-history memo.
\end{enumerate}
